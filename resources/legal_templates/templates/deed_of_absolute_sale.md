---
document_type: deed_of_absolute_sale
category: notarial
when_to_use:
  - "Transferring ownership of real or personal property for consideration, with the transaction complete/unconditional"
  - "Do NOT use for installment sales or sales with conditions precedent to transfer — use a Contract to Sell instead in those cases"
required_fields:
  - vendor_name_and_details
  - vendee_name_and_details
  - property_description
  - consideration_amount
notes: >
  Uses an ACKNOWLEDGMENT, not a jurat. Property description should be
  copied precisely from the title (OCT/TCT) or, for personal property, from
  the registration/proof of ownership document — do not paraphrase legal
  descriptions of real property, as precision here has legal consequences.
---

notes: >
  Uses an ACKNOWLEDGMENT, not a jurat (see conventions doc). Powers must be
  specifically enumerated — Philippine law requires a special (not general)
  power of attorney for acts like selling real property, so do not draft
  vague catch-all grants of authority for such acts; list them explicitly.

## SPECIAL POWER OF ATTORNEY

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

I, **{{PRINCIPAL_NAME}}**, of legal age, Filipino, [marital status],
resident of {{PRINCIPAL_ADDRESS}}, do hereby name, constitute, and appoint
**{{AGENT_NAME}}**, of legal age, Filipino, resident of
{{AGENT_ADDRESS}}, to be my true and lawful attorney-in-fact, for me and in
my name, place, and stead, to do and perform the following specific acts:

1. {{POWER_1, e.g. "To sell, transfer, and convey the parcel of land covered by TCT No. ___, situated at ___, to such buyer and for such price and terms as my attorney-in-fact may deem proper"}};

2. {{POWER_2, e.g. "To sign, execute, and deliver any deed of sale, transfer document, or other instrument necessary to effect the foregoing"}};

3. {{POWER_3, e.g. "To receive payment, issue receipts, and represent me before the Registry of Deeds, Bureau of Internal Revenue, and other relevant government agencies for purposes of the foregoing"}};

[Add or remove numbered powers as needed — do not include a general
catch-all grant beyond the acts actually intended to be authorized.]

HEREBY GRANTING AND CONFERRING upon my said attorney-in-fact full authority
to do and perform every act necessary to carry out the foregoing specific
powers, as fully as I might or could do if personally present, hereby
ratifying and confirming all that my attorney-in-fact shall lawfully do by
virtue hereof.

This Special Power of Attorney shall remain in full force and effect
until revoked in writing by the principal[, or until {{EXPIRATION_CONDITION, if applicable}}].

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this {{DATE}} at
{{PLACE}}.

_________________________
**{{PRINCIPAL_NAME}}**
Principal

Republic of the Philippines )

who executed the foregoing instrument, exhibiting to me [his/her]
{{ID_TYPE}} No. {{ID_NUMBER}}, issued on {{ID_ISSUE_DATE}} at
{{ID_ISSUE_PLACE}}, and acknowledged to me that the same is [his/her] free
and voluntary act and deed.

WITNESS MY HAND AND SEAL on the date and place first above written.

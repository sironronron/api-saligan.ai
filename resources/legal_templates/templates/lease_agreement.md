# CONTRACT OF LEASE

This Contract of Lease ("Lease") is entered into on **{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}**,
by and between:

**{{LESSOR_NAME}}**, [of legal age, Filipino / a corporation duly organized
under Philippine law], with address at {{LESSOR_ADDRESS}} ("Lessor");

AND

**{{LESSEE_NAME}}**, [of legal age, Filipino / a corporation duly organized
under Philippine law], with address at {{LESSEE_ADDRESS}} ("Lessee").

**WITNESSETH:**

## 1. PROPERTY LEASED
Lessor leases to Lessee the property described as: {{PROPERTY_DESCRIPTION}}
("Leased Premises").

## 2. TERM
This Lease shall be for a period of {{LEASE_TERM}}, commencing
{{START_DATE}} and ending {{END_DATE}}, [renewable upon mutual written
agreement of the parties / with an option to renew under the following
terms: {{RENEWAL_TERMS}}].

## 3. RENT
3.1 Lessee shall pay Lessor monthly rent of **{{MONTHLY_RENT}}**, payable
    in advance on or before the {{PAYMENT_DAY, e.g. "5th"}} day of each
    month.
3.2 Late payment shall accrue a penalty of {{LATE_PENALTY, e.g. "2% per month"}} on the overdue amount.
3.3 Rent shall be [fixed for the Lease term / subject to escalation of {{ESCALATION_RATE}} per year, effective {{ESCALATION_DATE}}].

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND ADVANCE RENTAL
Lessee shall pay Lessor, upon signing, a security deposit of
{{SECURITY_DEPOSIT}} and advance rental of {{ADVANCE_RENTAL}}. The
security deposit shall be applied to unpaid obligations or damage to the
Leased Premises upon termination, with any balance refunded to Lessee
within {{REFUND_PERIOD, e.g. "thirty (30) days"}} of turnover.

## 5. USE OF PREMISES
The Leased Premises shall be used solely for {{PERMITTED_USE}} and for no
other purpose without Lessor's prior written consent.

## 6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
{{MAINTENANCE_ALLOCATION, e.g. "Lessee shall maintain the interior in good condition at its own expense. Lessor shall be responsible for major structural repairs not caused by Lessee's fault or negligence."}}

## 7. UTILITIES
{{UTILITIES_ALLOCATION, e.g. "Lessee shall pay for electricity, water, and internet consumed on the Leased Premises."}}

## 8. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION
8.1 Failure to pay rent for {{DEFAULT_PERIOD, e.g. "two (2) consecutive months"}} shall constitute a material breach, entitling Lessor to terminate this Lease upon {{TERMINATION_NOTICE}} written notice.
8.2 Upon termination, Lessee shall vacate and surrender the Leased Premises in the condition received, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of the
Philippines[, including the Rent Control Act, to the extent applicable to
the Leased Premises].

## 10. SIGNATURES

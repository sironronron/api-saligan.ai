  the disputes this type of contract generates, so weak drafting here
  creates the most downstream risk.

**{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}**, by and between:

**{{CONTRACTOR_NAME}}**, [a corporation duly organized under Philippine law
/ an individual], with [principal office / address] at
{{CONTRACTOR_ADDRESS}} ("Contractor"), represented by
{{CONTRACTOR_REPRESENTATIVE}};

AND

**{{CLIENT_NAME}}**, [a corporation duly organized under Philippine law /
an individual], with [principal office / address] at {{CLIENT_ADDRESS}}
("Client"), represented by {{CLIENT_REPRESENTATIVE}}.

## 1. SCOPE OF WORK
{{SCOPE_OF_WORK_DESCRIPTION}}

## 2. TIMELINE
{{TIMELINE_OR_PHASES}}

## 3. COMPENSATION
Total contract price: **{{TOTAL_PRICE}}**, payable as follows:
{{PAYMENT_SCHEDULE}}

## 4. ACCEPTANCE
Client shall have **{{ACCEPTANCE_PERIOD, e.g. "ten (10) business days"}}**
from delivery to inspect and either accept or provide written notice of
material defects. Failure to provide written notice within this period
constitutes acceptance. Minor defects not affecting core functionality
shall not be grounds for withholding payment, provided Contractor remedies
such defects within {{CURE_PERIOD, e.g. "fifteen (15) business days"}}.

## 5. WARRANTY
Contractor warrants that deliverables will be free from material defects
for {{WARRANTY_PERIOD, e.g. "ninety (90) days"}} following acceptance.

## 6. PAYMENT TERMS
6.1 Invoices shall be issued upon delivery of each milestone.
6.2 Client shall pay each invoice within **{{PAYMENT_TERM, e.g. "thirty (30) calendar days"}}** of the invoice date.
6.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at **{{LATE_INTEREST_RATE, e.g. "1.5% per month"}}** on the outstanding balance, from the due date until full payment.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
{{IP_OWNERSHIP_TERMS, e.g. "Upon full payment, all work product transfers to Client. Prior to full payment, Contractor retains ownership."}}

## 8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon
{{TERMINATION_NOTICE_PERIOD, e.g. "thirty (30) days"}} written notice,
provided the breaching party fails to cure within such period.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential
damages. [Contractor/Client]'s total liability under this Agreement shall
not exceed {{LIABILITY_CAP, e.g. "the total fees paid"}}.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to keep confidential all proprietary business
information disclosed during the course of this engagement.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of the
Philippines.

## 12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
In the event of any dispute, the parties agree to first attempt resolution
through good-faith negotiation for {{NEGOTIATION_PERIOD, e.g. "fifteen (15) days"}}. If unresolved, the parties shall submit to
{{DISPUTE_MECHANISM, e.g. "mediation administered by a mutually agreed mediator"}} before either party may initiate litigation.
